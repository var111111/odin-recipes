Lucky Me! Beef Noodles

Description

Lucky Me! Beef Noodles is a quick and convenient Filipino-style instant noodle dish with a savory beef-flavored broth. It is commonly prepared by boiling the noodles with the included seasoning, making it an easy meal or snack when you want something warm and filling without much preparation.

The noodles have a soft, springy texture while the broth provides a salty, savory beef flavor. They can be eaten as prepared or customized with additions such as an egg, vegetables, or sliced meat.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack Lucky Me! Beef Noodles
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • Sliced vegetables or meat (optional)

Steps

  1. Poor 2 cups of water into a pot and bring it ot a boil.
  2. Add the Lucky Me! noodles.
  3. Cook for about 3 minutes, or until the noodles reach your preferred texture.
  4. Add the seasoning packets and stir well.
  5. If using an egg, crack it into the pot and cook until done.
  6. Add any optional vegetables or meat and cook until heated through.
  7. Transfer the noodles and broth to a bowl.
  8. Serve hot.
