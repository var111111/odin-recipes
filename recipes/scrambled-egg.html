Scrambled Eggs

Description

Scrambled eggs are a quick and comforting breakfast made with just a few simple ingredients. The eggs are whisked until smooth, then gently cooked in butter over low to medium heat. This method helps create soft, fluffy curds without overcooking them.

These scrambled eggs are easy to customize with your favorite seasonings or toppings. They can be served on their own, with toast, or alongside bacon, sausage, vegetables, or fresh fruit. The recipe makes a satisfying breakfast that can be prepared in just a few minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and add the milk, salt, and black pepper
  2. Whick the mixture until the eggs are well combined and slightly frothy
  3. Heat a nonstick pan over low to medium head and add the butter
  4. Once the butter has melted, pout the eggs into the pan
  5. Let the eggs sit for a few seconds, then gently stir them with a spatula folding the cooked portions toward the center
  6. Continue stirring and folding slowly until the eggs are mostly set but still slightly moist.
  7. Remove the pan from the heat immediately, as the eggs will continue cooking from the remaining heat.
  8. Serve hot with toast or your favorite breakfast sides.
