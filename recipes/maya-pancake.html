Maya Pancake

Description

Maya pancakes are a simple and delicious breakfast that can be prepared quickly using Maya pancake mix. The pancakes are soft, fluffy, and lightly sweet, making them enjoyable on their own or with your favorite toppings. They are perfect for a quick breakfast or afternoon snack.

These pancakes can be served warm with butter, syrup, honey, fruit, or other toppings. The recipe is easy to follow and only requires a few ingredients, making it a convenient choice for anyone who wants to prepare a tasty homemade breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Maya Pancake mix
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup water or milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • Butter for greasing the pan
  • Syrup or butter for serving(optional)

Steps

  1. Place the Maya pancake mix in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the egg, water or milk, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until the ingredients are combined. Do not overmix; a few small lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low to medium heat and lightly grease it.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter onto the pan for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges begin to look set.
  7. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until lightly golden.
  8. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  9. Serve warm with butter or syrup, or your preffered toppings.
